Beyond genetics, I apply statistical modeling to translate large-scale, multi-source data into
actionable risk assessments that inform public-health decision making at both the individual and
community level.

#### Highlights

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I led work developing individualized and community-level mortality
risk models for the United States, integrating information on pre-existing conditions,
socio-demographic factors, and community-level pandemic dynamics. The methods were deployed as
public-facing tools, including an online risk calculator and interactive risk maps.
