Modern biomedical data are high-dimensional and richly structured — genes interact within
pathways, features are correlated, and signals are often weak individually but coordinated
across a network. My work develops inference methods that exploit this known structure to
improve power and interpretability over approaches that treat features as independent.

#### Methods

**T2-DAG** is a powerful test for differentially expressed gene pathways. It uses graph-informed
structural equation modeling to incorporate the directed network structure among genes, yielding
substantially higher power to detect disease-associated pathways than structure-agnostic tests.
